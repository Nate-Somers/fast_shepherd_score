REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT, so not done

    kernel (0.3121 vs 0.4356 ms) and both together are 34.6% of the screen.
  - The ~2.6x ceiling on vol_lipo is refuted, not merely exceeded: measured 3.70x, with
    the whole array screen (0.405 s) below the 0.679 s wall the bound came from.

    independent curves show it flat: 580.4 / 612.8 / 575.0 a/s at N=1e3/1e4/1e5.
    Five hypotheses ruled out by direct measurement.
